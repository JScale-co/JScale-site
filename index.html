The JScale Story

We don't do "marketing." We book you real, high-ticket jobs.

If you run an epoxy or concrete flooring business, you've heard every promise in the book. Brand awareness. Impressions. Clicks. None of it pays your crew or puts a project on the calendar.

JScale came out of watching that exact problem up close.

Our founder saw it firsthand: a family member in real estate hired a traditional marketing team, and the flaw in the system became obvious fast. The agency did plenty of "marketing." It never actually multiplied revenue or closed a single extra contract. It was just noise.

Look around and you'll find the same pattern everywhere. Agencies take your money, hand you a generic report full of charts nobody acts on, and leave you to figure out the rest on your own.

We decided to build something different.

One industry. One job to do.

JScale works exclusively with high-ticket, blue-collar businesses, specifically epoxy and concrete flooring. We don't hand you raw, cold data and wish you luck. We stay in it until the job is booked.

We find your ideal clients.
Homeowners and commercial property owners who are actively looking for premium flooring work, not just browsing.
We generate real, qualified leads.
No junk forms, no tire-kickers. Every lead is checked before it reaches you.
We help close the deal.
We bridge the gap between marketing and actual revenue, so those leads turn into signed, high-ticket contracts on your books.

Your time belongs on the job site, running your crew and delivering floors that hold up. Not chasing internet leads who never pick up the phone.

JScale is your growth partner, not another marketing vendor. We handle the pipeline, we bring the jobs, and we help you scale your revenue. No fluff. No vanity metrics. Just more jobs on the books.
